about

I would not be who I am without my Lord and savior Jesus Christ, and the people who I spend time around.

I grew up in a small town 45 minutes outside Philadelphia called Coatesville, it's made me exactly who I am today. I was fortunate enough to go to a small school outside of Pittsburgh, PA called Robert Morris, where I got my Bachelor's degree in Sports Management from the Rockwell School of Business. Then, COVID-19 sent me home early my sophomore year and I fell in love with technology. Without an extra year (thanks COVID) I got my Master's Degree in Data Analytics.

This led me to Tampa, FL. A place I'm so fortunate to call home. I worked for the Tampa Bay Rays for almost 4 years, building out production data applications and dashboards on the Strategy & Analytics team. #RaysUp

I still remember talking to ChatGPT for the first time in late 2023 in my single bedroom apartment in Tampa. From that moment, I saw my vision, I wanted to help other people use the most transformative technology the world has ever seen. So that's what I'm doing now, helping businesses automate processes with AI.

Above all, I love God and I love people. This drives me.
